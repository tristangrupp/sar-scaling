Round 2: inland scenes and a change mask

Round 2 adds 25 inland Stripmap scenes (158 usable tiles) to the 396 round 1 tiles. The pilot set leaned on islands and coasts, because Stripmap since 2023 is acquired mostly there. Round 2 searches the whole ASF archive (19,180 Stripmap SLCs since 2014) for cells more than 25 km inland with a same-direction IW pass within 6 days on Planetary Computer. Chicago, São Paulo, the Amazon and Campinas have many Stripmap scenes but no same-direction IW within 6 days, so they could not be used.

The 25 new scenes cover 13 regions on four continents. Training regions: Darién and Venezuelan tropical forest; Western Sahara and Mauritanian desert; the Tibesti mountains; eastern Yemen; Iranian arid mountains and towns; Sahel cropland and towns (Niger, Kano); Lake Chad; Amur cropland and forest; and Montreal. Two new regions are held out whole. The near holdout is the western Yemen highlands around Sanaa and Saada (cities, terraces), about 200 km from the eastern Yemen training cells. The far holdout is Bangladesh (dense villages, paddy), more than 2,000 km from any training tile. No training tile lies within 40 km of a held-out tile. All 25 scenes register to their same-direction IW pass, with scene offsets of 3 to 38 m and median 10 m correlation of 0.33 to 0.86. All 158 tiles are usable. Round 1 tiles and their splits stay as they were (splits.csv is unchanged; splits_v2.csv copies its assignments).

Change mask. For every tile, the mask uses the IW passes on the paired IW's track (same relative orbit) just before and just after the Stripmap date, normally 11 to 12 days apart (24 days where only one satellite flew). A 10 m pixel counts as changed when its 3×3-mean backscatter differs by more than 3 dB between those two passes. A first attempt allowed any same-direction pass. It flagged 20 to 36% of Owens, Togo and the paper's scenes, because other tracks see slopes and towns from another incidence, so the mask now uses one track only. In stable deserts (Tibesti, Western Sahara) the mask flags 0.3% of land with a pass-to-pass spread of 0.9 dB, so the threshold sits above the speckle floor there. Most regions flag 4 to 8%, which is close to the speckle tail at their 1.5 to 1.7 dB spread, so part of it is noise and not change. High values mark real change: Montreal in February (snow) 20%, the paper's Tyrol and Franconia winter scenes 21%, eastern Yemen 18% and Lake Chad 14%. 44 tiles (the newest 2026 scenes) have no later pass yet and carry no mask. Each mask is stored per tile in data/change/<tile_id>.npz (change10 and diff_db at 10 m) and listed in change_v2.csv. It is not yet applied to any training loss; training on v2 waits for the GPU.

Land-cover mix. Round 2 shifts the set toward bare and sparse ground (42% of round 2 pixels against 8% in round 1) and roughly doubles the cropland share (11% against 6%). Water drops from 14% to 3%. Across both rounds the set is now 22% built-up, 22% tree cover, 18% bare, 14% grassland, 8% cropland and 11% water. Cropland is still thin: the inland cells with dense Stripmap acquisition and same-direction IW are mostly arid. Chicago and São Paulo, the obvious cropland and city cells, have no same-direction IW within 6 days.
