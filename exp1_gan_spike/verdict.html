No. At 4x, neither model recovers real X-band detail from Sentinel-1 IW. Both learn to remove speckle and output a smooth image that matches X-band brightness patterns at 10 m and below. They do not draw buildings, streets or rail lines that the X-band shows.

The numbers say the same thing. On held-out tiles the models carry only 6–7% of the real fine-detail energy (hf_ratio 0.06–0.07). The detail they do add barely lines up with the real detail (detail_r 0.05, where 0 is random). Their lower error than bicubic comes from smoothing out speckle, not from sharpening.

The GAN stage changed nothing. Its discriminator separated real from generated almost perfectly from the start, and at an adversarial weight of 0.01 the generator ignored it. Outputs are visually identical to the pixel-loss model. A stronger adversarial weight would add texture, but with detail_r near zero that texture would be invented.

This supports the change of plan: aim for 20 m to 5 m against same-sensor Stripmap, and use X-band for geometry checks only.
