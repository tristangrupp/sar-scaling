Neither tested model recovered the fine structure visible in the X-band reference. Both produced smoother images with fewer speckle fluctuations. Buildings, streets and rail lines that were absent from the input remained unresolved.

The original held-out evaluation found 6–7% of the reference fine-detail energy and detail correlation of about 0.05. The summary table reflects the later run, so its values differ slightly. Lower pixel error mainly reflected smoothing rather than recovered detail.

The GAN fine-tune produced nearly the same output as the pixel-loss model. The discriminator separated real and generated images early in training, and an adversarial weight of 0.01 had little effect. This run provides no evidence that the GAN recovered additional structure.
